Welcome to the official mobile application of the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Gauriganj (Amethi / Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh) Alumni Association — JNVGFamily!

Dedicated to the eternal Navodaya spirit of "Never Ending Relations" (हमी नवोदय हो), this non-profit voluntary platform connects alumni across batches, current and retired gurujans (faculty), and mentors from 1993 to 2026.

🌟 KEY FEATURES OF THE APP:

• 🎓 VERIFIED ALUMNI DIRECTORY
Browse alumni profiles across batches, houses (Arawali, Nilgiri, Shivalik, Udaigiri), and professional domains — including Civil Services, Software & Tech, Defense & Police, Medicine & Healthcare, and Entrepreneurship.

• 📜 MEMORY WALL & COMMUNITY BROADCAST
A vibrant nostalgic social board where alumni share morning assembly memories, mess nostalgia, sports meet memories, hostel stories, and campus photographs live with the entire fraternity.

• 🏛️ 4TH ANNUAL MEGA MEET 2026 HEADQUARTERS
Get live countdowns, route guidelines, event schedules, RSVP confirmations, and stage itinerary for the upcoming mega reunion.

• 🎤 CULTURAL PERFORMANCE REGISTRATION
Register your solo song, nostalgic skit, poetry, or musical performance for the Annual Meet cultural night.

• 🌸 GURU VANDANA & FACULTY SALUTE
Connect with retired and active teachers, read their blessings, and honor our mentors who shaped our lives in the residential campus.

• 🤝 ALUMNI AID & GIVING BACK
Support student scholarships, campus infrastructure grants, and development funds with 100% transparent audit records.

• 🔒 PRIVACY FIRST & ROLE-BASED SECURITY
Strict institutional verification, masked phone numbers, 2FA authentication, and secure data handling.
