Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 19, 2026 • Version 0.0.1

This Privacy Policy applies to the JNV Alumni Platform & Mobile Application (available on the web at jnvgfamily.in and on Android / iOS devices). The platform is a voluntary, non-profit community initiative dedicated to uniting the alumni, faculty, and well-wishers of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Gauriganj, Dist. Amethi / Sultanpur (U.P.).

1. Institutional Non-Profit Purpose

The JNV Gauriganj Alumni Network operates strictly as a non-commercial, non-profit voluntary fraternity registered for solidarity, student mentorship, and rural education support. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal information to data brokers, advertisers, or third-party marketing companies.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate fraternal connections, verify alumni credentials, and organize reunions, we collect the following information submitted voluntarily by users:

  • Profile & Identity Data: Full Name, Passout Batch Year (1993–2026), School House (Arawali, Nilgiri, Shivalik, Udaigiri), Current City, and Professional Designation.
  • Contact & Verification Data: Phone Number (used for WhatsApp/SMS 2FA verification simulations) and Email Address. Phone numbers and emails are masked in public views to protect members against unsolicited communication.
  • Community & Event Contributions: Memories, hostel nostalgia, messages, and photographs posted to the Memory Wall; Cultural performance registrations for the Annual Alumni Meet; Suggestions and issue reports.
  • Voluntary Donation References: For voluntary contributions to the Alumni Corpus / Vidyalaya Development Fund, UPI reference IDs and transaction amounts are recorded for accounting transparency and annual audit by registered chartered accountants. We do not store sensitive payment credentials such as debit/credit card numbers or UPI PINs.

3. Mobile App & Device Permissions

The mobile application requests the minimum necessary runtime permissions:

  • Photos & Storage: Optional access required only if you choose to attach alumni nostalgia photos or upload a profile picture. The app never accesses photos outside of those you explicitly select.
  • Internet Access: Required to synchronize the live alumni directory, Memory Wall broadcasts, and event notifications.

4. Role-Based Security & Data Access Controls

Our platform enforces rigorous Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

  • Unauthenticated guests cannot access phone numbers, emails, or directory profiles.
  • Alumni directory details are accessible only by verified members of the Navodaya Parivar.
  • Financial ledgers and donor lists are restricted to authorized Association Admins, the 1996 Host Committee, and Financial Moderators.
  • All web and mobile communication is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS (Let's Encrypt SSL).

5. Data Deletion & Account Erasure Requests

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies and global privacy standards, every alumnus has the absolute right to view, modify, or permanently delete their account and associated data at any time.

How to request data deletion:

You can request instant deletion of your profile, posts, and contact information by:

  • Sending an email from your registered address to: ashfaque15tr@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Alumni Account".
  • Submitting a request through the "Suggestion / Issue Report" dialog in the platform footer.

Upon receiving your request, our technical admin team will permanently purge your personal profile, phone numbers, and memory posts from our database within 48 business hours.

6. Children's Privacy

The JNV Alumni Platform is intended for alumni who have completed their secondary or senior secondary education at Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, as well as current and retired teachers. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
