Our Roots in Gauriganj (Est. 1986)

Established in the heart of Gauriganj (formerly Sultanpur, now District Amethi), Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya stands as a premier residential institution under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Over three decades, the campus has nurtured hundreds of talents who now lead in Civil Services, Medicine, Corporate Leadership, Defense, Academics & Education, and Global Technologies.

Non-Profit Voluntary Network: Registered for education & solidarity. Dedicated to uniting alumni across batches, mentoring students, and supporting the Alma Mater.

The Four House System

Every Navodayan belongs to one of four houses during their 7-year residential journey:

Arawali Old Name: Ashok House
Shivalik Old Name: Raman & Shivaji House
Udaigiri Old Name: Subhash House
Nilgiri Old Name: Tagore House
Historical Note: There was previously Sarojini House and Priyadarshini House for girl students, which later unified with the four houses (Arawali, Shivalik, Udaigiri, Nilgiri).

🛡️ नवजीवन सहायता मंच • Multi-Tier Alumni Support System

Alumni Sahayata & Escalation Matrix

Structured fraternity care: When an alumnus requires help (medical emergency, career mentorship, educational relief, or financial distress), it is first handled within their Batch Circle (Tier 1). If unresolved, it escalates to the Regional Chapter (Tier 2), and ultimately to the Apex Central Committee & Welfare Fund (Tier 3).

01
Tier 1 • Primary Intake

Tier 1: Member / Batch Level Intake

Assistance is first evaluated within your own batch peers and batch coordinators. Fast resolution for career advice, initial contributions, and local contact within 48–72 hours.

✓ Peer-to-Peer Solidarity
02
Tier 2 • Regional Mobilization

Tier 2: Moderator Review & Mobilization

If the batch cannot fulfill the requirement, the request escalates to the Regional Chapters (Lucknow, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Amethi) and House networks for cross-batch aid.

✓ Regional Hospital & Legal Logistics
03
Tier 3 • Apex Central Intervention

Tier 3: Governance Admin & Welfare Fund

In high-impact emergencies or major medical treatments, the Central Executive Committee and Apex Welfare Fund release direct financial grants and launch an official association appeal.

✓ Audited Central Disbursement
