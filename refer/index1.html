Our Roots in Gauriganj (Est. 1992)

Established in the fertile lands of Gauriganj (then part of Sultanpur, now Amethi District), Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya stands as a testament to the National Policy on Education 1986. Over 3 decades, the campus has nurtured hundreds of rural talents who now lead globally.
